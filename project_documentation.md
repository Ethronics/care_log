# Log My Care – Project Documentation

**Version:** 1.0 – Smart Edition

## Purpose

To replace paper-based care management with an intelligent, efficient, and user-friendly digital platform.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Log My Care – Smart Edition** is a digital companion for care homes and home care agencies. It enables care providers to manage their entire operation—from staff scheduling to minute-by-minute care recording—within a single, unified platform.

**Guiding Principle:**

> **Less Paperwork, More Care**

By automating administrative workflows and simplifying data entry, the platform allows carers and managers to focus on delivering high-quality, person-centered care.

---

## 2. Target Users & Roles

### The Carer (Front-line Staff)

**Needs**

* Clear visibility of assigned shifts and visits
* Simple task lists for each service user
* Fast, low-effort care logging

**Pain Points**

* Time-consuming handwritten notes
* Missed or delayed updates about service users
* Uncertainty around shift schedules

**Solution**
A mobile-first application that acts as a digital assistant, guiding carers through their day and capturing care records effortlessly.

---

### The Manager (Administrator)

**Needs**

* Full shift coverage with appropriately skilled staff
* Oversight of staff compliance and availability
* Confidence during audits and inspections

**Pain Points**

* Manual rota creation ("Rota Tetris")
* Chasing staff for updates and paperwork
* Limited real-time visibility into care delivery

**Solution**
A centralized **Mission Control Dashboard** that automates scheduling, highlights risks, and provides instant operational insight.

---

## 3. Key Feature Overview

### A. Smart Rota Builder

Managing staff schedules is one of the most resource-intensive tasks in care operations.

**Standard Capability**

* Drag-and-drop calendar for manual shift assignment

**Smart Enhancement: Auto-Fill Scheduling**

* Managers click a single **Auto-Fill** button
* The system evaluates:

  * Staff availability
  * Required skills and qualifications
  * Contracted working hours
* Produces a complete, balanced rota where:

  * All shifts are covered
  * No staff member is overworked
  * Skill requirements are met automatically

---

### B. Intelligent Daily Logging

Care logging is a legal and regulatory requirement.

**Standard Capability**

* Manual text entry via mobile forms

**Smart Enhancement: Voice-to-Text Reporting**

* Carers tap a microphone icon and speak naturally
* Spoken notes are instantly transcribed into professional care records
* Ideal for hands-busy or time-sensitive situations

---

### C. The Care Office (Service User Hub)

A single digital record for every individual receiving care.

**Core Components**

* **Profile:** Medical history, preferences, emergency contacts
* **Care Timeline:** Real-time feed of all care events, meals, medications, and observations
* **Family Portal (Optional):** Secure, read-only access for family members to receive reassurance and updates

---

### D. Leave & Absence Management

Ensures staff availability data is always accurate.

**Features**

* Holiday requests submitted via the app
* Instant logging of sudden leave (e.g., sickness)

**Smart Integration**

* Sudden leave automatically triggers rota gap detection
* Auto-Fill suggests the most suitable replacement based on skills and availability

---

### E. Payroll & Hours Tracking

Eliminates manual errors in pay calculation.

**Capabilities**

* Contracted hours tracking per staff member
* Comparison of planned vs. actual worked hours
* Real-time pay estimation, including:

  * Overtime
  * Special shift rates

---

## 4. A Day in the Life

### Morning: The Manager

**08:00 AM**

* Logs into the Care Office Portal
* Dashboard alert:

  > *"Sarah (Nurse) reported sick at 07:30 AM"*

**System Actions**

* Shift marked as **OPEN**
* Three qualified replacement suggestions generated automatically

**Manager Action**

* Clicks **Auto-Fill**
* David is assigned based on availability and qualifications

**Outcome**

* Schedule updated instantly
* David receives a push notification:

  > *"New shift assigned: Today 09:00 – 17:00"*

---

### Afternoon: The Carer

**02:00 PM**

* Arrives at service user’s home
* Opens the app and views Afternoon Visit task
* Warning flag displayed:

  > *"User missed morning medication"*

**Care Delivered**

* Medication administered
* Lunch prepared

**Care Logged (Voice Input)**

> *"Administered afternoon meds. Prepared ham sandwich, user ate half. Mood is calm."*

**Outcome**

* Record saved instantly
* Manager sees the update in real time

---

## 5. Why This Matters

By combining **Smart Scheduling** and **Voice-First Logging**, Log My Care significantly reduces technical friction in care delivery.

**Key Benefits**

* **Efficiency:** Managers save hours each week on scheduling
* **Accuracy:** Voice input encourages richer, more timely care notes
* **Safety:** Real-time visibility allows issues to be identified immediately

Ultimately, the platform enables care organizations to operate with greater confidence, compliance, and compassion.
